\noindent{\large\bfseries\color{navy}1.\ Summary of Major Revisions and Verified Scientific Findings}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=3pt,topsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{Replaced Degenerate Single-Token Attention ($T=1$) with Genuinely Sample-Dependent Multi-Token Attention (\texttt{MHA\_128}) and Gating (\texttt{GatedFusion}):} Directly resolving Reviewer 1's core critique, we replaced $T=1$ cross-attention with three-token multi-head self-attention (\texttt{MHA\_128} / \texttt{TokenAttentionFusion} over $[\mathbf{x}_{\text{cls}}, \operatorname{LN}(\mathbf{x}_s), \operatorname{LN}(\mathbf{x}_f)]$, $L=3$, 199,425 trainable parameters) and explicit sample-dependent \texttt{GatedFusion} (41,987 parameters). Quantitative weight logging confirms genuine per-domain modulation (e.g., \texttt{GatedFusion} shifts from $23.1\%$ RGB / $76.9\%$ Wavelet on unseen LFW reals to $79.0\%$--$83.7\%$ RGB / $16.3\%$--$21.0\%$ Wavelet on unseen diffusion fakes).
    \item \textbf{Eliminated Capacity Confounds, Validation Leakage, and Format Shortcuts:} We curated a verified \textbf{556,082-image} balanced benchmark (\texttt{DatasetMultiDomain\allowbreak Clean\_v2}: 4 native high-resolution authentic datasets with 66\% PNG / 34\% JPG + 15 diffusion/swap generators under a Leave-One-Generator-Out split), enforced out-of-sample cross-fitted Phase 2 fusion training (\texttt{fusion\_train}: 58,103; \texttt{fusion\_dev}: 12,376; untouched \texttt{seen\_test}: 12,729), and benchmarked three 1:1 parameter-matched controls (\texttt{ConcatMatchedMLP}, \texttt{NoAttnFFN}, \texttt{LinearMix}) plus a 16-configuration MLP sweep across four seeds with identity-cluster bootstrap 95\% CIs, paired Cohen's $d$, and Holm--Bonferroni correction (Table~8).
    \item \textbf{Programmatically Audited Performance, External Baselines, and Honest Limitations:} Every confusion matrix, Macro-F1, ROC-AUC, EER, Brier score, and 15-bin ECE was recomputed via a single unit-tested script (\texttt{tools/paper1\_\allowbreak revision\_\allowbreak suite.py}). On untouched \texttt{seen\_test}, \texttt{MHA\_128} achieves \textbf{99.12\% $\pm$ 0.01\% accuracy} (0.9997 AUC, 0.87\% EER, significantly lower Brier score than \texttt{GatedFusion} at $p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0021$) and ranks \#1 on strictly native PNG-only controls (\textbf{98.90\%}, 1.04\% EER), while explicitly reporting two-way authentic-vs-synthetic trade-offs ($+6$ spatial FPs traded for $-54$ spatial FNs, while cutting wavelet FPs by 61.1\%). On four untouched unseen domains under matched JPEG $Q=85$ (LFW vs.\ LDM, SDXL\_Refine, DiffFace, StyleGAN), staged decoupled fusion decisively defeats unfrozen joint end-to-end training ($4.5\%$ LFW real recall) and fine-tuned \texttt{Xception} ($43.04\%$ mean unseen accuracy), while our $12\times$ smaller 25.5M \texttt{GatedFusion} (\textbf{85.71\% $\pm$ 0.06\%} mean unseen accuracy; \textbf{95.40\%} DiffFace; \textbf{97.7\%} JPEG $Q=30$) outperforms 304M \texttt{UniversalFakeDetect} (CLIP ViT-L/14) on \texttt{seen\_test} and all three unseen diffusion/swap domains. We also added \texttt{ConvNeXt\_128} modularity with matched fusion controls ($+9.86\%$ unseen gain for \texttt{ConvNeXt + MHA\_128}), exact GPU-inclusive latency ($24.92\,$ms, $\approx 40$ FPS), FairFace demographic fairness ($0.00\%$ FPR disparity), and an honest end-to-end raw-pixel FGSM sweep across all five models reporting full real/fake recall collapse.
    \item \textbf{Redrawn Figures, Line Numbers, and Expanded Literature Positioning:} All seven figures were redrawn at 300 DPI (clean architecture diagram matching Table~1, paired train/val convergence curves, ROC/PR/reliability diagrams, 4-seed bars with separate EER axis, perturbation/FGSM curves, single-row t-SNE with Silhouette/Fisher scores, and male-only synthetic Grad-CAM with $[0,1]$ colorbar and masking controls), continuous line numbers (\texttt{\textbackslash lineno}) were enabled, and Related Work was expanded with FAViT (2026), LGF-Net (2026), FreqDebias (CVPR 2025), UniversalFakeDetect (CVPR 2023), and DRCT (ICML 2024).
\end{itemize}
