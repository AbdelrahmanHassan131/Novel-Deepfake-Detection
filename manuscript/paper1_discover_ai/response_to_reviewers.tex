\noindent Dear Editor (Dr.\ Eugenio Vocaturo), Assistant Editor (Paras Ukhalkar), and Reviewers 1 and 2,

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent We sincerely thank the editorial team and both reviewers for their thorough, rigorous, and constructive evaluation of our manuscript. Every technical, methodological, statistical, and presentation concern raised in the reports has been carefully addressed through substantial new experiments, architectural upgrades, programmatic verification of every metric and confusion matrix, redrawn figures, and textual revisions. Line numbers have been enabled throughout the revised manuscript (\texttt{\textbackslash lineno}) for convenient verification.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Summary of Major Revisions:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Replaced degenerate single-token attention ($T=1$)} with genuinely sample-dependent three-token multi-head self-attention (\texttt{MHA\_128} over $[\mathbf{x}_{\text{cls}}, \mathbf{x}_s, \mathbf{x}_f]$, 199,425 parameters) and sample-dependent \texttt{GatedFusion} (41,987 parameters), and verified dynamic per-domain modality modulation quantitatively.
    \item \textbf{Eliminated capacity confounds:} Added three 1:1 parameter-matched ($\approx$198K--199K) controls (\texttt{ConcatMatchedMLP}, \texttt{NoAttnFFN}, \texttt{LinearMix}) plus a 16-configuration concatenation width/dropout sweep across $N=4$ seeds with Holm--Bonferroni multiplicity correction.
    \item \textbf{Eliminated validation-selection bias, producer--consumer overfitting, and format shortcuts:} Built a verified 556,082-image multi-domain benchmark (4 native high-resolution authentic datasets + 15 balanced diffusion generators) with a Leave-One-Generator-Out (LOGO) split, out-of-sample cross-fitted Phase 2 fusion training, untouched \texttt{seen\_test} ($n=12,729$), strictly native PNG-only controls, and four untouched external unseen domains (LFW vs.\ LDM, SDXL\_Refine, DiffFace, StyleGAN) under matched JPEG $Q=85$ recompression.
    \item \textbf{Empirically validated staged decoupling} against an unfrozen joint end-to-end dual-stream training baseline (which collapsed to $4.5\%$ unseen real recall).
    \item \textbf{Programmatically audited every table, confusion count, calibration metric (Brier/ECE/reliability diagrams), end-to-end raw-pixel FGSM sweep, nine real-world perturbations, FairFace demographic fairness audit, and exact latency (incl.\ vs.\ excl.\ GPU DWT).}
\end{itemize}

\section*{Point-by-Point Response to Reviewer 1}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 1 (Single-token attention collapse in Eq.\ 13)}{With sequence length $T=1$, softmax is identically $1.0$, contradicting the adaptive-modulation motivation. Either implement genuinely sample-dependent weighting (e.g., gating or multi-token inputs) or remove the adaptive-modulation claim.}
\authorresponse{We fully agree and have resolved this contradiction directly in the architecture, title, equations, and experiments. We replaced degenerate $T=1$ cross-attention with \textbf{genuinely sample-dependent three-token multi-head self-attention} (\texttt{MHA\_128} / \texttt{TokenAttentionFusion} over $[\mathbf{x}_{\text{cls}}, \operatorname{LN}(\mathbf{x}_s), \operatorname{LN}(\mathbf{x}_f)]$, $L=3$, Eqs.\ (4)--(9)) and also implemented \textbf{explicit sample-dependent \texttt{GatedFusion}} predicted from the concatenated embeddings. Empirical weights confirm genuine input-dependent modulation: \texttt{GatedFusion} shifts from $23.1\%$ RGB / $76.9\%$ Wavelet on unseen LFW reals to $79.0\%$--$83.7\%$ RGB / $16.3\%$--$21.0\%$ Wavelet on unseen diffusion fakes, and \texttt{MHA\_128} shifts $[\text{CLS}]$ attention from $33.0\%$ RGB / $38.1\%$ Wavelet on LFW reals to $46.2\%$ RGB / $19.0\%$ Wavelet on StyleGAN.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 2 (Unverified gradient starvation / modality dominance claim)}{Add a jointly trained end-to-end multi-branch baseline or remove the assertion.}
\authorresponse{We added an empirical \textbf{Joint End-to-End Unfrozen Dual-Stream Control} (ResNet-50 + Wavelet CNN + \texttt{MHA\_128} trained jointly unfrozen on 80,000 balanced training images; Table~5). Joint end-to-end training collapsed severely on unseen real photographs (LFW real recall $=4.5\%$, unseen accuracy $=52.10\%$ on LDM/DiffFace, $52.15\%$ on SDXL\_Refine, and $12.20\%$ / $0.0515$ AUC on StyleGAN), whereas staged decoupling maintained $90.3\%$--$98.5\%$ unseen real recall and $84.71\%$--$85.71\%$ four-domain unseen mean accuracy.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 3 (Attribution of new vs.\ adopted components and per-component parameters)}{State explicitly at the start of Section 3 which components are new vs.\ adopted and extend Table 1 with per-component parameter counts.}
\authorresponse{Added a dedicated subsection \textit{Attribution of Adopted vs.\ Newly Introduced Components} at the start of the Proposed Framework section and expanded Table~1 to list exact parameter counts for every adopted trunk (ResNet-50: 23,508,032; Wolter Conv1--4: 1,661,760) vs.\ every newly introduced bottleneck and fusion head (199,425 for \texttt{MHA\_128}; 41,987 for \texttt{GatedFusion}).}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 4 (Fourfold parameter mismatch between MHA and Fusion\_128)}{Add (i) a parameter-matched pure-MLP concatenation head, (ii) an FFN-only ablation without attention, and (iii) a linear-mixing control without softmax.}
\authorresponse{All three exact 1:1 capacity-matched controls were implemented (\texttt{models/mha/ablation\_heads.py}) and trained across $N=4$ seeds (Tables~1, 3, and 5): \texttt{ConcatMatchedMLP} (198,017 params; $0.993\times$), \texttt{NoAttnFFN} (198,657 params; $0.996\times$), and \texttt{LinearMix} (198,785 params; $0.997\times$) vs.\ \texttt{MHA\_128} (199,425 params). We report the candid finding transparently: on untouched \texttt{seen\_test}, capacity-matched heads are statistically indistinguishable ($99.12\%$--$99.16\%$, all $0.9997$ AUC, $p_{\text{Holm}}>0.15$); on strictly PNG-only controls and unseen calibration, \texttt{MHA\_128} leads ($98.90\%$ PNG accuracy, $\text{ECE}=0.039$), while lightweight \texttt{GatedFusion} (41,987 params) achieves the best 4-domain unseen accuracy ($85.71\% \pm 0.06\%$) and DiffFace transfer ($95.40\%$).}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 5 (Same 20\% split used for model selection and final reporting)}{Adopt a strict three-way split with an untouched test subset.}
\authorresponse{Implemented a strict three-way identity-grouped partition: Phase 1 experts train on 439,222 LOGO training images; Phase 2 fusion heads train on out-of-sample \texttt{fusion\_train} ($n=58,103$) with checkpoint selection strictly on \texttt{fusion\_dev} ($n=12,376$); and final reporting uses untouched \texttt{seen\_test} ($n=12,729$) plus four completely untouched external unseen benchmarks ($n=1,000$ per domain).}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 6 (Producer--consumer overfitting when training fusion on the experts' training split)}{Report train/validation gap or adopt a cross-fitted protocol.}
\authorresponse{We adopted a strict \textbf{out-of-sample cross-fitted stacking protocol}: Phase 2 fusion heads are trained exclusively on held-out images ($n=58,103$) that neither Phase 1 expert ever saw during training. We also report the exact train--dev ROC-AUC gap for every architecture ($-0.00055$ for \texttt{MHA\_128}, $-0.00051$ for \texttt{ConcatMatchedMLP}, $-0.00056$ for \texttt{GatedFusion}) and full train/dev convergence curves (Figure~2).}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 7 (Asymmetric residual formulation in Eq.\ 7 and Linear(256$\to$128) control)}{Justify the residual formulation and add a single-direction Linear(256$\to$128) control.}
\authorresponse{Replaced the old cross-domain residual with standard Transformer self-attention residuals (Eqs.\ (7)--(8)) over $[\mathbf{x}_{\text{cls}}, \mathbf{x}_s, \mathbf{x}_f]$, and added both \texttt{NoAttnFFN} ($\text{Linear}(256\to 128)+\text{FFN}$) and \texttt{LinearMix} controls in Tables~3 and 5.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 8 (No per-generator or per-source decomposition)}{Add per-generator recall/AUC and per-source authentic recall.}
\authorresponse{Added complete per-source authentic recall (CelebA, FairFace Val, FFHQ-256) and per-generator recall and ROC-AUC across all 13 seen generators in Table~4, plus all 4 unseen domains (LDM, SDXL\_Refine, DiffFace, StyleGAN) in Table~5.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 9 (Exclusion of LDM / Stable Diffusion subsets and undocumented quota)}{Document sampling rules and use excluded generators for leave-one-generator-out evaluation.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 10 (CelebA-dominant authentic shortcut risk)}{Provide a full source-by-class contingency table, recall stratified by authentic source, and a same-pipeline control.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 11 (No published external baselines / state-of-the-art positioning)}{Reproduce published baselines or explicitly disclaim global state-of-the-art claims.}
\authorresponse{We both added \textbf{two published external baselines} evaluated under the identical \texttt{seen\_test} and unseen JPEG $Q=85$ protocol (Tables~3 and 5) \emph{and} explicitly disclaimed global SOTA claims over large foundation detectors: (i) \textbf{\texttt{Xception}} (FaceForensics++, R\"{o}ssler et al., ICCV 2019), which reaches $99.18\%$ on \texttt{seen\_test} ($0.63\%$ EER) yet collapses to $43.04\%$ mean unseen accuracy ($7.0\%$ LFW real recall); and (ii) \textbf{\texttt{UniversalFakeDetect}} (Ojha et al., CVPR 2023; frozen 304M CLIP ViT-L/14 linear probe), which achieves $98.05\%$ on \texttt{seen\_test} ($2.01\%$ EER) and $68.90\%$ on unseen StyleGAN, while our 12$\times$ smaller 25.5M \texttt{GatedFusion} outperforms it on \texttt{seen\_test} ($99.10\%$) and all three unseen diffusion/swap domains (\texttt{LDM} $96.45\%$ vs.\ $94.95\%$, \texttt{SDXL\_Refine} $97.35\%$ vs.\ $95.30\%$, \texttt{DiffFace} $95.40\%$ vs.\ $94.90\%$).}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 12 (Unsupported explanation for Fusion\_128 AUC drop; missing curves and sweep)}{Report train/val loss and AUC curves for both heads and a capacity/dropout sweep.}

\reviewercomment{R1 Comment 14 (ConvNeXt evaluated without concatenation counterpart)}{Report both fusion variants per backbone or remove the unverified comparison.}
